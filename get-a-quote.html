How it works

  1. Describe the project

    What you're building or fixing, roughly how big, and where on the lot. The more detail, the more accurate the price.

  2. Local pros take a look

    Your request goes to excavation professionals who work in Lehigh Acres. Most will want a site visit before giving a firm price — digging quotes depend on ground conditions no one can see from a form.

  3. Compare the quotes

    You get pricing directly from the pros — usually within one business day. No obligation, no pressure.

What to include for an accurate price

The four numbers that decide your price are depth, volume, water, and haul distance. Mention how deep the dig goes, roughly how much ground it covers, whether the area holds water after rain, and whether the dug-out dirt can stay on the lot — your quotes will be far more accurate with all four in the description.
